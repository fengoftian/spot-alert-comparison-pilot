# Prospective Binance Spot alert comparison — v1

Owner authorization: "proceed with your proposal", 2026-10-09 NZDT. This is a new public-data, simulated-account screening experiment; no live orders/accounts/credentials, no retired-strategy changes, no protected local history reads.

**Question:** Do prior-range breakout context, a same-time-of-day participation baseline, or five completed minutes of price persistence improve after-cost whole-account screening outcomes over the simple 10%/3x detector?

## Fixed comparisons, before prospective data

Completed 1-minute candles only. Features use only information available at decision time.
- A0: trailing 60-minute close return >=10%, AND trailing 60-minute USDT turnover >=3x mean hourly turnover over the preceding, non-overlapping 24 hours.
- A1: A0 AND current close strictly above the highest high of that preceding 24 hours (excluding the current signal hour).
- A2: A1's price/structure conditions, replacing its volume baseline with the median turnover of the corresponding trailing hour on the preceding seven UTC days; ratio >=3. No requirement that A0's volume condition also hold.
- A3: A2, then five subsequent completed minute closes all at or above the signal-hour traded VWAP (sum quote turnover / sum base volume); if any are missing or fail, reject. Only after confirmation can entry be attempted.

Comparisons are A1–A0, A2–A1, A3–A2. No parameter search, no altered exits, no ensemble optimization, no best-rule promotion from an interim result. Volatility-normalized price gating and slower ensembles are deferred to a distinct future experiment to keep this comparison interpretable. The parameters are research design choices, not paper-proven optimal settings.

## Point-in-time universe and events

Before the start, capture the public Binance Spot symbol list and 24h ticker list. Screen a candidate pool of at most 50 active Spot/USDT pairs with quote turnover >=2m USDT, ranked by turnover descending then symbol, excluding a fixed list of stable/fiat/wrapped bases. OGN/RLC are included only if they qualify; no forced inclusion. Before freezing, remove primary-source-identified stablecoins, gold-backed tokens and tokenized equities from that inception candidate pool, without replacement or prospective performance inspection. Preserve the initial UNIVERSE.json and use the resulting COHORT.json for the 30-day pilot; classification exclusions and sources are in CLASSIFICATION.json. This conditions inference on the inception cohort, not all historical/current Spot markets. New listings are not added retrospectively. BTC/ETH are separately collected for benchmarks whether or not they qualify as tradable cohort members.

Rising-edge qualifying events only; one attempted event per symbol/model per rolling 24 hours, including rejected entries. Maintain qualification state through warmup: conditions already true at study start are left-truncated and do not generate artificial initial entries. Stream every completed minute in order. Missing data are recorded; late/backfilled signals never create retrospective live-book fills. Event counts are clustered by symbol/UTC day for descriptive sample reporting; variants and overlapping horizons are not independent samples.

## Timing, execution evidence and fixed accounts

A0–A2 planned entry: first public-book observation at or after signal close +60 seconds, no later than +180 seconds. A3 planned entry: confirmation close +60 seconds, within the same 120-second grace. Each book is captured once and reused across the applicable variants/capital points. No historical order book is invented. Entry observations must have request duration <=5 seconds and a received timestamp within the allowed entry interval. A retry within the entry interval is permitted; outside it the entry is missed/rejected. Reject malformed, crossed or insufficient books, unavailable symbols, failed lot/minimum-notional filters, or executable book cost above 50 bps per side after a 50% displayed-depth haircut. Books indicate hypothetical availability, not actual fills or queue position. Network latency is measured request timing, not order latency.

Separate continuous simulated accounts for each model, each authorized capital (250 / 2000 / 2500; 2000 primary), and each slippage scenario (15 / 50 / 300 bps per side). Same maximum cash clip for a given capital across all models/scenarios: risk fraction 0.25% divided by (6% price-stop distance +0.2% round-trip fees +0.3% baseline round-trip shortfall) = 3.8461538% of starting capital. This conservative new research sizing is frozen before validation and will not be raised to rescue results. Nominal baseline stop-risk allowance: 0.625 / 5 / 6.25 USDT at 250 / 2000 / 2500, not a guaranteed loss cap. No compounding-up of clips; two simultaneous positions maximum, no averaging down, no borrowing.

Simulated entry quantity is floored to frozen LOT_SIZE using the 300bps scenario price plus fee for the cash ceiling, so the three scenarios share the same rounded quantity; this can reduce deployment below the clip ceiling. Commission assumed 0.10% per side in USDT, not account-verified. Simulated fill is book midpoint plus/minus max(scenario shortfall, measured book cost per side). Thus spread/impact is not double-counted; 300 bps is not rejected because it exceeds the observed-book gate. Liquidation costs are always applied to already-open positions. Exchange lot/minimum-notional constraints still apply to exits; unexecutable dust remains unresolved, while entry cost/fee gates never block risk-reducing exits. This is a scenario model, not a stress qualification or real fill.

Common exit: first observed completed minute close <=94% of the entry midpoint, otherwise at four hours after the entry timestamp. No take-profit/trailing-rule fitting. Exit uses the first subsequently observed valid book (even if late), with delay recorded. If an exit book is unavailable, keep the position marked and pending; never fabricate a cash exit. At the 30-day cutoff, no new entry; stop collection only after open positions settle, with a maximum extra four-hour observation tail. Unsettled positions remain explicitly open/unresolved at that tail, not realized winners/losses.

Account breaker: 3% continuous close/book-marked drawdown (7.50 / 60 / 75 USDT from starting capital as an initial peak) triggers liquidation of all positions and permanent entry halt. It is a containment rule; gaps may overshoot. The binding objective remains <=15% max drawdown, 300 USDT from a 2000-USDT peak. Missing marks cannot establish drawdown compliance; all stale periods are flagged.

Maximum 24 entries per model/account in the 30-day pilot (a stricter proportional implementation of <=300/year). Fee allowance 3% *30/365 of starting capital; no new entry after actual fees plus reserved two-sided fee estimates reach it. Risk-reducing exits are never blocked by the budget; any overshoot is reported as a budget breach, not waived. No short-window fee/trade annualization is used as a measured rate.

## Collection and completion

Capture seven-day-plus-one-hour minute warmup from public Binance market-data-only GET endpoints into a new database. Warmup is not validation data. Seal rule/code/universe identities before a future start; choose the next five-minute boundary at least five minutes after freeze. End is exactly 30 days later. Public requests are restricted to time, exchangeInfo, ticker/24hr, klines and depth. No signed request path or key environment is read.

A local launchd collector runs every minute, with a single-process lock, bounded pagination and disk cap. It can recover historical candles after downtime; it cannot recover missed live books. It requires the Mac to be awake and online. Registration and bar receipts must be checked; an idle scheduler tick is not collection evidence. A Codex follow-up, if configured, inspects receipts and completes the fixed report, without modifying rules or granting live authority.

Simultaneous entry priority is scheduled entry time, then symbol lexicographically, independently for each alternative account. Freeze records exact start/end UTC and Auckland times, universe, specification and code hashes. A hash mismatch stops collection; corrections require a new version, never edits to a frozen generation. Logs/raw evidence and runtime state are append-only or transactional/idempotent; derived status files may be regenerated.

Report at completion: continuous whole-account period return and drawdown pair, deployment, fees/slippage, idle cash with zero-yield assumption plus matched cash/BTC diagnostics, capital-scale differences, counts of independent event clusters, rejected/missed entries, stale marks, coverage gaps and unresolved positions. No CAGR for this 30-day screening window. Require at least 20 accepted symbol/day event clusters across the union before treating comparisons as more than INCONCLUSIVE. This minimum is a screening convention, not a power guarantee. No formal qualified inference, full-cycle benchmark pass or live qualification is claimed. Stop after the fixed window; no silent extension or retuning.
